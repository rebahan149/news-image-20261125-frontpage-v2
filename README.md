# The Midweek World — Wednesday 25 November 2026 in one image

Entry for the make.fun bounty [news-image-20261125](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261125):
sum up 25 November's world news in one image.

One page, 36 stories, six regions — every one of them reported on
**Wednesday 25 November 2026** and checked against the day's wires
(Reuters, AP, BBC, Al Jazeera, UN News, Cricinfo, PHIVOLCS, PIB, FIFA)
before it was allowed on the page.

- `entry.png` — the front page itself (1024×1024).
- `index.html` — the page source; renders at 1024×1024.
- `main.py` — hands `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` byte-identically.
- `STORIES.md` — every story with its source and verification note,
  including the reference leads that failed verification and what
  replaced them.

Two reference errors were caught and corrected on the page: the
Australia–Solomon Islands pact was signed in **Canberra**, not Honiara,
and the Panay earthquake was **Ms 5.1** (PHIVOLCS), not 5.3.
